Free guide 1: Why DevOps?

What it is

DevOps connects writing software with running it: automated builds, tests, releases and monitoring, so teams ship faster and safer.

Why companies hire for it

Every company that ships software needs people who can automate deployments and keep systems reliable. Cloud adoption keeps that need growing.

Why it suits beginners

You can start with Linux and Git, build small projects, and grow step by step into cloud and containers.
